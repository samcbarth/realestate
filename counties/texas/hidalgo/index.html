Hidalgo County, Texas: investor resources

Last checked 2026-11-09 against official county and state sources

Hidalgo County is a Texas county with an estimated 921,549 residents (Census Bureau, July 1, 2025); its county seat is Edinburg. Like every Texas county, it holds foreclosure and tax sales on the first Tuesday of the month between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. Property values come from the appraisal district, and foreclosure notices are filed with the county clerk.

How sales work in Hidalgo County

Texas sets the sale rules statewide, so they are the same in every county:

  • Mortgage foreclosures are usually nonjudicial trustee sales. Notice must be posted at the courthouse and filed with the county clerk at least 21 days before the sale (Property Code 51.002). Texas foreclosure guide
  • Sales are held on the first Tuesday of the month between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m. at the courthouse or an area the commissioners court designates, or online if the county authorizes it.
  • Tax sales follow a court judgment or tax warrant and are run by the sheriff or a constable. Homestead and ag property can be redeemed for 2 years, other property for 180 days (Tax Code 34.21). Texas tax sale guide

This page does not list auction dates or properties. Sale lists change every month; get them from the county offices above or the officer conducting the sale.
