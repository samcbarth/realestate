Rogers County, Oklahoma: investor resources

Last checked 2026-11-09 against official county and state sources

Rogers County is an Oklahoma county with an estimated 102,197 residents (Census Bureau, July 1, 2025); its county seat is Claremore. Its county treasurer holds the annual tax resale on the second Monday of June, and mortgage foreclosures there usually end in a court-ordered sheriff's sale after appraisal and published notice.

How sales work in Rogers County

Oklahoma sets these rules statewide; each county's treasurer, sheriff and district court carry them out:

  • Land with taxes unpaid 3 or more years is sold at the county treasurer's resale on the second Monday of June (68 O.S. 3105, 3125). Oklahoma tax sale guide
  • Mortgage foreclosures usually go through district court and end in a sheriff's sale held at least 30 days after the first published notice (12 O.S. 764). Oklahoma foreclosure guide
  • Unless appraisal was waived, sheriff's sale bids must reach two-thirds of the appraised value, and the court must confirm the sale. Oklahoma sheriff sale guide

This page does not list auction dates or properties. Sale lists change every month; get them from the county offices above or the officer conducting the sale.
