Oklahoma real estate investor guides

Oklahoma mortgage foreclosures usually run through the district court and end in a sheriff's sale, while tax-delinquent land goes to the county treasurer's annual resale held the second Monday of June. These guides explain each sale type and link to the Oklahoma statutes.

Oklahoma tax sales

Oklahoma tax sales explained for investors: land delinquent 3 or more years goes to the county treasurer's resale on the second Monday of June, in person or online.

Oklahoma foreclosure process

Oklahoma foreclosure for investors: judicial foreclosure and sheriff's sale, the Power of Sale Act's 35-day cure notice, the homestead election, appraisal and 30-day sale notice.

Oklahoma probate property

How inherited real estate is transferred in Oklahoma and what buyers should check: summary administration up to $200,000, the $50,000 small estate affidavit and who can sign the deed.

Oklahoma sheriff sales

Oklahoma sheriff sales explained: court-ordered sales after foreclosure, appraisal by three disinterested people, the two-thirds minimum bid, 30-day published notice and court confirmation.
