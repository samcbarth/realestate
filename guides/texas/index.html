Texas real estate investor guides

Texas sells foreclosed and tax-delinquent property at public auctions held on the first Tuesday of each month, between 10 a.m. and 4 p.m., at the county courthouse or another place the commissioners court designates, or online where the county authorizes it. These guides explain each sale type and link to the Texas statutes.

Texas tax sales

Texas tax sales explained: who holds them, the first-Tuesday schedule, online auctions, minimum bids, struck-off resales and the 2-year or 180-day redemption period.

Texas foreclosure process

The Texas nonjudicial foreclosure process for investors: the 20-day notice to cure on homesteads, 21-day notice of sale, first-Tuesday courthouse auctions and what buyers should check.

Texas probate property

How inherited property is transferred in Texas and what that means for buyers: the 4-year will deadline, muniment of title, small estate affidavits and affidavits of heirship.

Texas sheriff and constable sales

Texas sheriff and constable sales explained: what gets sold (execution and tax foreclosure sales), the first-Tuesday schedule, online auctions and a pre-bid checklist.
