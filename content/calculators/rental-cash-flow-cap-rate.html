How do you calculate rental property cash flow?

Monthly cash flow is what is left after every cost of owning the property, including the loan. The calculator works through it in this order:

  1. Gross income = (monthly rent + other income) x 12
  2. Effective gross income (EGI) = gross income minus the vacancy allowance
  3. Operating expenses = property tax + insurance + HOA + owner-paid utilities + maintenance, management and CapEx reserves (each a percentage of rent)
  4. Net operating income (NOI) = EGI minus operating expenses
  5. Cash flow = NOI minus yearly principal and interest, then divided by 12 for the monthly figure

How do you calculate cap rate?

Cap rate = NOI / purchase price x 100. It answers one question: if you bought the property with cash, what yearly return would its income produce? Because it leaves out financing, two investors with different loans see the same cap rate on the same property.
