How do you calculate gross rental yield?

Gross yield = (monthly rent x 12) / purchase price x 100. It is fast and needs only two numbers, which makes it useful for sorting a long list of listings. It ignores every expense, so two properties with the same gross yield can perform very differently.

How do you calculate net rental yield?

Net yield = (yearly rent - yearly operating expenses) / (purchase price + purchase costs) x 100. Including purchase costs, such as closing costs and any immediate repairs, gives a truer picture of the money tied up in the property.

The gap between the two numbers is the cost of running the property. If you only ever look at gross yield, that gap is the part you are not seeing.

When to use yield instead of cash flow

Use yield to compare properties quickly, before you have loan terms. Once you know how you will finance the purchase, switch to the cash flow and cap rate calculator to see the monthly result after the mortgage.
